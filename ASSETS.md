# Asset and font provenance

ErdenVaux is an original fictional brand created in November 2026. Product information, prices, dimensions and stock are illustrative fixtures. No private client code, imagery or business records are included.

## Photography

All24 local WebP files are original generated assets: ten canonical product/variant images and two campaign portraits, each with a responsive small derivative. [IMAGE_PROVENANCE.md](docs/IMAGE_PROVENANCE.md) and [image-prompts.json](docs/image-prompts.json) document generation, exact dimensions, prompts and hashes. The media owner verified all twelve records and twenty-four outputs before handoff.

Products are1200×1500 and640×800; campaigns are1200×1800 and640×960. Both campaign portrait frames are preserved. Every product/variant photo matches the approved silhouette, material, colour and silver-tone hardware. A gallery detail is explicitly a crop of the same photo, never an invented additional angle.

The app serves its own local files under the GitHub Pages subpath. It does not hotlink or embed a screenshot as the interface. Screenshot evidence in `docs/screenshots/` is captured from the actual application.

## Typography

- **Bodoni Moda:** Latin variable WOFF2 from `@fontsource-variable/bodoni-moda@5.3.0`,46,260 bytes. [SIL OFL license](docs/licenses/Bodoni-Moda-OFL.txt).
- **Manrope:** Latin variable WOFF2 from `@fontsource-variable/manrope@5.3.0`,24,836 bytes. [SIL OFL license](docs/licenses/Manrope-OFL.txt).

These are self-hosted through Next's local font support. No external font service is contacted. Exactly two intentional font families are used; the original code license does not relicense fonts or third-party dependencies.

## Design research

Official [Celine](https://www.celine.com/en-us/women/handbags/), [Lemaire](https://www.lemaire.fr/collections/women-bags) and [Mansur Gavriel](https://www.mansurgavriel.com/) pages informed restrained navigation, editorial spacing and product presentation. Lemaire and Mansur Gavriel were visually inspected; Celine's browser capture was blocked, with its official HTML navigation inspected instead. No assets, products, copy or brand claims were copied. ErdenVaux's paired portrait campaign and licensed type pairing are original to this portfolio project.

Original SVG interface icons and the centimeter-scaled comparison illustrations are code-native artwork. The code uses the MIT license; generated photography is documented separately rather than blanket-relicensing other parties' work.
